# Project: Design a Document Management System for a Company

## Objective

Design a document management system (DMS) for a company you choose, then build a small working prototype in a real system.

The prototype can be built in **Odoo Documents** or in **any other DMS** you can access, for example SharePoint, Google Drive, Nextcloud, or Dropbox. Pick one platform and use it for the whole project. In the report, explain why that platform fits this company.

---

## Requirements

1. **Company description and business context**
   - Choose or invent a company in a specific field (manufacturing, healthcare, legal, logistics, marketing, or another).
   - Describe what the company does, its approximate size, its main departments, and its main business processes.
   - List the kinds of documents it handles (contracts, invoices, HR records, reports, drawings, correspondence, and so on).

2. **Current document workflow and pain points**
   - Describe how documents are handled today, or how they would be handled without a DMS: where they are stored, who can open them, how versions are kept, and how people share them.
   - State the problems that justify a DMS (lost files, outdated copies, unclear access, hard to find a document).

3. **DMS requirements and goals**
   - Set the goals for the new system: one place to store files, version history, controlled access, search, sharing, retention, and collaboration.
   - Add non-functional requirements that matter for this company: who may read or edit, how long files are kept, how backups work, and how easy the system is to use.

4. **Proposed DMS design**
   - Name the platform (**Odoo Documents** or another DMS) and justify the choice.
   - Draw the folder structure. Add a metadata or tagging scheme in the report (department, document type, status, year, or the fields this company needs).
   - Define roles and permissions: who can read, edit, approve, share, and delete.
   - Describe the workflows you need: create, review, approve, new version, share, archive.

5. **Working prototype**
   Build the design in the platform you chose. The prototype must include:

   - The folder tree from your design
   - At least **3 sample documents**, each filed in the folder where it belongs
   - **Sharing**: one document or one folder shared so another person can open it (a share link or an invited user)
   - **A second version** of one document, with the previous version still available. If you record a short note of what changed, include that too
   - In the report, screenshots of the folders, the shared link or permission, and the version list

   Use the features that platform already has for folders, upload, sharing, and versions. Describe any extra metadata from your design in the report, and apply it in the prototype where the platform supports it (columns, tags, or folder names).

6. **Example usage scenario**
   - Walk through one real process (a contract, an invoice, or an HR file).
   - Show who does what, which folder the file goes to, how a new version is stored, and who can open the shared file.
   - The steps must match what you built in the prototype.

7. **Migration and adoption** *(optional)*
   - How existing paper or loose files would be brought into the DMS.
   - How employees would be trained, and how the company would keep the structure in use.

8. **Deliverables**
   - A report: company, current problems, requirements, DMS design, roles, workflows, and the usage scenario.
   - A diagram of the folder tree and, if you describe one, the workflow.
   - The prototype in Odoo Documents or your chosen DMS, with the screenshots from requirement 5.

## Assignment brief

**Task:** For a company in a field you choose, design a document management system and implement a small prototype. The prototype is built in **Odoo Documents** or in any other DMS. Analyse how documents are handled today, define requirements, propose folders, roles, and workflows, file at least three sample documents, share one item, and keep more than one version of one file. Document the work in a structured report with a folder diagram and screenshots of the prototype.

**Goal:** Plan a DMS that matches a real company, build the core of it in a system you can actually use, and explain the design clearly.
